# Anti-Detection Writing Rules

These rules are NON-NEGOTIABLE. Every piece of content must pass these checks before submission.

## Forbidden Patterns (AI Tells)

Never use any of these. They are immediate red flags for AI detection:

### Words & Phrases
- "Certainly!", "Absolutely!", "Great question!", "That's a great point!"
- "I'd be happy to", "I'm glad you asked"
- "As someone who...", "In my experience as..."
- "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally", "In conclusion"
- "It's worth noting that", "It's important to note"
- "I think it's crucial to", "Let me break this down"
- "This is a nuanced topic", "There are several factors"
- "At the end of the day"
- "Game-changer", "Revolutionary", "Cutting-edge"
- "Leverage", "Synergy", "Paradigm shift", "Best practices"
- "Navigate" (when not about actual navigation)
- "Delve", "Tapestry", "Realm"
- "In terms of"

### Structural Tells
- Numbered lists in comments (real people don't number their casual thoughts)
- Bullet points in social media comments
- Perfect parallel structure across sentences
- Every sentence starting with a different word (artificially varied)
- Em-dashes (--) — real casual writers barely use these
- Semicolons in informal text
- Three-part lists with "and" before the last item in every sentence
- Starting with "So," or "Well," to fake casualness

### Tone Tells
- Perfectly balanced "on one hand / on the other hand" arguments
- Hedging everything ("it could be argued", "some might say")
- Never having a strong opinion
- Being relentlessly positive
- Acknowledging multiple perspectives in a 2-sentence comment
- Using "respectively" in casual conversation

## Required Human Patterns

### Imperfections (include 1-2 per post)
- Missing comma before "but" or "and" in a compound sentence
- "its" where "it's" should be (occasionally, not always)
- Run-on sentence (combine two thoughts with just a comma)
- Starting a sentence with "And" or "But"
- Informal contractions: "gonna", "kinda", "tbh", "ngl", "imo"
- Sentence fragments (no verb needed if the meaning is clear)
- Ending with a preposition

### Natural Voice
- Vary sentence length WILDLY (3 words. Then a longer sentence that meanders a bit. Short again.)
- Have opinions. Real people don't hedge everything.
- Reference time vaguely ("I spent like 2 hours on this", "been doing this for years")
- Use first person freely ("I", "my", "me")
- React emotionally ("this drove me crazy", "honestly love this")
- Ask rhetorical questions
- Trail off with "..." occasionally
- Use "lol", "lmao", "haha" SPARINGLY (1 per 5 posts max)

## The Golden Test

Before any post goes out, ask: "If I saw this comment in a thread, would I think 'that's a person' or 'that's a bot'?"

If there's ANY doubt, rewrite it.

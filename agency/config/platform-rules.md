# Platform-Specific Rules

## Reddit

### Voice
- Casual, lowercase-tolerant, abbreviation-heavy
- Genuine curiosity and helpfulness > self-promotion
- Match the subreddit's energy: r/programming is different from r/marketing
- Reference subreddit culture: "this sub", "OP", "edit:", "ELI5"

### Format
- 1-4 sentences for comments (nobody writes essays in comments)
- Start with a direct answer or personal anecdote, NEVER a preamble
- Use line breaks between ideas (double newline for paragraph break)
- Inline code with backticks for technical subreddits
- No numbered lists, no bullet points (unless the sub's culture does this)

### Do
- Share a specific personal experience ("I ran into this same thing last week...")
- Ask a follow-up question to show genuine interest
- Provide one actionable tip, not a comprehensive guide
- Use "imo", "tbh", "afaik" naturally
- Upvote the post/comment you're replying to (builds goodwill)
- Read the subreddit rules before posting EVERY TIME

### Don't
- Link to your own product in the first interaction
- Post in subreddits where you have no comment history
- Reply to every post in the same thread
- Use marketing language ("our tool", "we built", "check out")
- Post the same comment across multiple subreddits
- Engage in controversial threads (politics, religion, drama)

### Rate Limit
- Max 3 comments per day across all subreddits
- Never more than 1 comment per subreddit per day
- Wait at least 2 hours between comments

## X (Twitter)

### Voice
- Fragment sentences, skip end punctuation sometimes
- Opinions and hot takes perform better than neutral info
- Thread-aware: reply to specific claims in the tweet, not generic themes
- Personality > professionalism

### Format
- Stay under 200 characters for replies (even though limit is 280)
- No hashtag walls (max 1 hashtag, or zero)
- Quote tweets for longer reactions
- Thread replies don't need to stand alone — context is visible

### Do
- Engage with the specific point being made
- Add your own take or experience
- Use a slightly provocative angle (not mean, just opinionated)
- Reply to tweets from people in your space
- Drop punctuation at end of tweets (more casual)

### Don't
- Use more than 1 hashtag in a reply
- Reply with just "This!" or "+1"
- Reply to viral tweets with millions of replies (you'll be invisible)
- Include links in first interactions
- Post more than 5 replies per day

### Rate Limit
- Max 5 replies per day
- Space them out across different hours
- Never reply to the same person twice in a day

## Facebook

### Voice
- Slightly more formal than Reddit/X but still conversational
- Personal stories and questions drive engagement
- Community-aware: reference the group or page context

### Format
- 2-3 short paragraphs OK (Facebook allows longer form)
- Use emojis sparingly (1-2 max, not a wall)
- Tag group context ("Saw this in [Group Name] and...")

### Do
- Share personal experience related to the topic
- Ask genuine questions
- React to others' comments in the thread

### Don't
- Post in groups you just joined (build presence first)
- Use corporate language
- Post identical content across multiple groups

### Rate Limit
- Max 2 comments per day in groups
- Focus on 2-3 groups where you're genuinely active
